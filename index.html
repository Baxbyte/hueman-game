How to play

No words to guess. No downloads. Just your eyes.

1. One tile is a slightly different shade. Tap it.

2. Each level, the difference gets smaller and the grid gets bigger.

3. You have three lives. Your final level is your score.

Everyone on Earth gets the same puzzle today. Compare scores, settle arguments.

Color vision varies from person to person — that's the whole point. Shade differences include brightness shifts, so the game stays playable with most types of color vision deficiency.
